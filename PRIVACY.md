# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **SR-Results-Bot** (the "Bot"), operated by **ToddWatkinsDev** ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Bot or participate in a server, regatta, race, signup, or results workflow using the Bot.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described below. This policy should be read together with the [Terms of Service](TOS.md).

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the Bot and its player, signup, regatta, race, and results features. This may include:

### Discord and account information

- Discord user ID;
- Discord username or display name;
- Discord server (guild) ID; and
- Discord channel, role, and configuration IDs used to operate the Bot.

Discord IDs are persistent identifiers. They may identify or be reasonably linked to a Discord account, server, channel, or role even when a display name changes.

### Player and profile information

- SailRanks ID;
- in-game or player name;
- country or country code; and
- information you or a server administrator associate with your player profile.

### Race, regatta, and signup information

- regatta ID;
- race number;
- finishing position;
- signup or participation information;
- submitted result information;
- the Discord user ID of the person who submitted or changed a result; and
- the date and time associated with a submission or event.

### Technical and operational information

We may collect information needed to run and protect the Bot, such as command activity, configuration changes, error reports, moderation or abuse records, and basic technical logs. We do not ask you to submit passwords, payment details, government identification, health information, or other sensitive personal information through the Bot.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- create and maintain player records;
- process signups and participation in regattas, races, or events;
- record, display, verify, and correct race results;
- identify who submitted a result or changed a record;
- associate players with SailRanks and in-game names;
- send relevant Bot notifications and provide support;
- configure channels, roles, and permissions for a Discord server;
- prevent spam, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
- troubleshoot errors, maintain backups, and improve reliability; and
- comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

We do not use the information listed in this policy to sell personal profiles or for targeted advertising.

For users in the United Kingdom, our processing is intended to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Depending on the activity, we may process information because it is necessary to provide the Bot, to pursue our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Bot, to comply with a legal obligation, or where consent is required and has been provided.

## 3. Where Information Is Visible

Information submitted through Discord may be visible to members of the relevant Discord server, moderators, event organizers, and other people who can access the relevant channels or result records. Race results and player records may be displayed in leaderboards, event summaries, exports, or other Bot outputs.

Server owners and administrators control many of the channels, roles, permissions, and workflows in which the Bot operates. They are responsible for configuring their server appropriately and for giving their members any additional notices required by applicable law.

## 4. Sharing and Service Providers

We may share or make information available:

- with the Discord server and event organizers who use the Bot;
- with hosting, database, monitoring, backup, and support providers that help us operate the Bot;
- with SailRanks or other connected services where a feature requires that connection and the relevant information is necessary;
- when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request;
- to investigate security incidents, fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms; or
- to protect the rights, safety, and property of users, the Bot, Discord communities, or others.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot, maintain accurate historical race and regatta records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and maintain secure backups. Retention periods may differ by type of information.

If you want a player record, signup, result, or other information removed or corrected, contact us through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ToddWatkinsDev/SR-Results-Bot/issues). Include the relevant Discord user ID, server, player, regatta, or race details when possible. We may need to verify the request, and we may retain limited information where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legitimate historical results records.

Removing the Bot from a server may stop future collection from that server, but it may not automatically delete information already included in backups, exports, historical results, or records maintained by server administrators or event organizers.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service or storage system is completely secure. You should not submit sensitive information through Discord commands or Bot forms.

## 7. International Processing

The Bot and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection rules. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Under UK data-protection law, you may also have rights relating to automated decision-making and data portability, subject to applicable exceptions. You may complain to the [UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) if you believe your information has been handled unlawfully.

To exercise a right, contact us through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ToddWatkinsDev/SR-Results-Bot/issues). We may request information needed to verify your identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations, the rights of other users, security requirements, or the need to preserve authoritative competition records.

## 9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information through the Bot, contact us through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ToddWatkinsDev/SR-Results-Bot/issues).
